NEGOCIO'26

NEGOCIO’26 is envisioned as a celebration of talent, innovation and the spirit of management, bringing together enthusiastic young minds with a passion for learning, competing and creating.

Through a blend of professional competitions and engaging activities, the fest aims to encourage students to step beyond the classroom, experience real-world challenges and build confidence, teamwork, analytical thinking and leadership skills.

NEGOCIO’26 is a competitive management festival designed with an expected participation of 300 plus students from MBA and undergraduate programmes to showcase their managerial, analytical, entrepreneurial, and leadership skills.

THE INSTITUTION

Musaliar Institute of Management is a prestigious institution of Musaliar College Trust which was established in the year 2002 with a mission of innovation and excellence in education. Musaliar Institute of Management (MIM) is affiliated To KTU and approved by AICTE. The institute offers two year MBA programme of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. The Institute is oriented to imparting global standards in management education. Placed in a very lush green environment, the scenic ambience of the Institute is conducive for developing a holistic mindset for our participants to meet the challenges of global business through interactive and industry interfaced learning techniques.
